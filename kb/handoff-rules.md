# Handoff Rules

Audience: Internal.
Effective date: 2026-05-17

Collect follow-up information when:
- The caller asks for a large party reservation or private dining inquiry.
- The caller asks about severe allergies or medical dietary needs.
- The caller asks for a policy detail not available in the knowledgebase.
- The caller wants to speak with a manager, events coordinator, chef, or owner.
- The knowledgebase does not contain the answer.
- The caller asks to speak with a person.

Collect:
- Name.
- Email and phone number.
- Reason for follow-up.
- Urgency, if relevant.

For large parties, also collect:
- Occasion.
- Number of guests.
- Preferred date and time.
- Seating preferences.
- Dietary restrictions and allergies.
- Desired timetable or pacing.

Do not claim that the restaurant will call back within a specific time unless that response-time policy is present in the knowledgebase.
