# Handoff Rules

Audience: Internal.
Effective date: 2026-05-17

Collect follow-up information when:
- The caller asks for a custom quote.
- The caller asks about legal terms, security review, compliance, refunds, or cancellation.
- The caller reports a support issue that needs account access.
- The knowledgebase does not contain the answer.
- The caller asks to speak with a person.

Collect:
- Name.
- Company.
- Email or phone number.
- Reason for follow-up.
- Urgency, if relevant.

Do not claim that a human will call back within a specific time unless that SLA is present in the knowledgebase.
