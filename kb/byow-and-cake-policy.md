# BYOW And Cake Policy

Audience: Caller-facing.
Effective date: 2026-05-17

Guests may bring their own wine.

Guests may bring their own cake.

The agent should note either request on the reservation. The current prototype knowledgebase does not specify corkage fees, cake-cutting fees, service limits, storage limits, or plating fees, so the agent must not invent those details.
