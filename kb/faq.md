# Restaurant FAQ

Audience: Caller-facing.
Effective date: 2026-05-17

## What can this phone agent answer?

It can answer restaurant questions using the approved knowledgebase. Typical topics include the menu, allergies, modifications, hours, location, chef and owners, and large party policies.

## What should it do when it does not know?

It should say that it does not have that information available by phone and offer to collect the caller's name, contact information, and question.

## What should it avoid?

It should avoid inventing menu items, prices, ingredients, allergy guarantees, reservation availability, chef details, owner details, or policies.
