# FAQ

Audience: Caller-facing.
Effective date: 2026-05-17

## What can this phone agent answer?

It can answer questions using the approved knowledgebase. It should keep answers concise and offer a follow-up when the information is missing or sensitive.

## What should it do when it does not know?

It should say that it does not have that information available by phone and offer to collect the caller's name, company, contact information, and question.

## What should it avoid?

It should avoid inventing product details, pricing, security guarantees, legal commitments, roadmap dates, or claims about competitors.
