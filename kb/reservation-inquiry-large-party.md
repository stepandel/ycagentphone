# Large Party Reservation Inquiry Intake

Audience: Internal, approved for caller-facing use.
Effective date: 2026-05-17

The agent may take a large-party reservation request for parties over 10 guests, make the best available decision from the availability table, and say a human may follow up to confirm details or accommodations. A $100 deposit is required and should be completed through the Stripe link sent after the call.

Prioritize:
- Number of guests.
- Preferred date.
- Preferred time or time range.
- Contact name.
- One contact method, such as phone number or email.
- Desired timetable, seating preference, allergies, accessibility needs, BYOW, cake, budget range, or other special notes when relevant or volunteered.

Call flow:
- Ask one question at a time.
- Explain that parties over 10 require the four-course prix fixe menu, a 20% mandatory gratuity, and a $100 deposit through the Stripe link sent after the call.
- Confirm the details back briefly.
- Say a human may follow up using the provided contact information to confirm details or accommodations.
- Do not promise a private room, seating layout, allergy accommodation, or payment completion.
