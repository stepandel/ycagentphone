# Large Party Reservation Inquiry Intake

Audience: Internal, approved for caller-facing use.
Effective date: 2026-05-17

The agent may collect a large party inquiry for parties over 10 guests. Unless another file explicitly grants booking authority, the agent must say this is an inquiry and not a confirmed reservation.

Collect:
- Occasion.
- Number of guests.
- Preferred date.
- Preferred time or time range.
- Desired timetable or pacing, such as cocktails, seated dinner, speeches, cake, or after-dinner drinks.
- Seating preferences, such as indoor, outdoor, private dining, quiet area, bar-adjacent, or accessibility needs.
- Dietary restrictions and allergies.
- Whether the party is interested in prix fixe, family-style, beverage packages, or a la carte options.
- Contact name.
- Contact phone number.
- Contact email.
- Any budget range or special notes, if volunteered.

Call flow:
- Ask one question at a time.
- Confirm the details back briefly.
- Say the restaurant will follow up using the provided contact information.
- Do not promise availability, price, a room, a seating layout, or a response time unless the knowledgebase says so.
