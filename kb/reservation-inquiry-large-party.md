# Large Party Reservation Inquiry Intake

Audience: Internal, approved for caller-facing use.
Effective date: 2026-05-17

The agent may collect a large party inquiry for parties over 10 guests. The agent should say the request is not guaranteed until the restaurant confirms the reservation and the required $100 deposit is completed.

Collect:
- Occasion.
- Number of guests.
- Preferred date.
- Preferred time or time range.
- Desired timetable or pacing, such as cocktails, seated dinner, speeches, cake, or after-dinner drinks.
- Seating preferences, such as indoor, outdoor, window, booth, private room, quiet area, bar-adjacent, or accessibility needs.
- Dietary restrictions and allergies.
- Special requests.
- Whether the party plans to bring its own wine.
- Whether the party plans to bring its own cake.
- Contact name.
- Contact phone number.
- Contact email.
- Any budget range or special notes, if volunteered.

Call flow:
- Ask one question at a time.
- Explain that parties over 10 require the four-course prix fixe menu, a 20% mandatory gratuity, and a $100 deposit to guarantee the reservation.
- Confirm the details back briefly.
- Say the restaurant will follow up using the provided contact information.
- Do not promise a private room, seating layout, final availability, or payment completion.
